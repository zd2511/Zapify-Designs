The following terms apply to the Zapify Designs Website Subscription.

1. Domain Registration

  • The first year of domain registration is included in the subscription.
  • Domain renewal fees after the first year will be communicated to the client before renewal.
  • Domain renewals must be paid to keep the domain active.
  • Domain registration and renewal remain subject to domain registrar requirements and availability.

2. Business Email

  • Business email services are included in the subscription.
  • The number of mailboxes and storage allocated will be specified when the subscription is agreed upon.
  • Additional mailboxes or storage requirements may incur additional charges.
  • Business email services are subject to the email provider's terms and storage limitations.

3. Subscription Period & Cancellation

  • A minimum subscription period will apply, as specified in the client's subscription agreement.
  • Clients must provide written notice of cancellation in accordance with the agreed cancellation notice period.
  • Outstanding subscription fees and additional charges must be settled before cancellation is finalised.
  • Cancellation will result in the termination of subscription-based hosting, maintenance and included services.

4. Website Ownership & Access

  • Websites developed under the R650 monthly subscription are hosted and managed by Zapify Designs.
  • While the subscription is active, Zapify Designs manages the website hosting environment and its underlying website files.
  • Clients will not receive direct access to the website's hosting account, server environment or website files while using Zapify Designs' hosting services.
  • If a client decides to move to another hosting provider, Zapify Designs will gladly provide the website files and/or assist with transferring the website to the new hosting provider at no additional cost.
  • The client may request a website transfer when changing hosting providers, subject to settling any outstanding amounts.
  • Third-party services, licences, premium plugins, subscriptions and proprietary software may be subject to their respective licensing and transfer restrictions.

5. Website Maintenance

  • Unlimited reasonable website maintenance is included while the subscription remains active.
  • This includes routine content updates, text corrections, image replacements, minor layout adjustments, troubleshooting and general website upkeep.
  • Major redesigns, entirely new pages or sections requiring substantial development, new functionality, custom integrations and additional application development are excluded.
  • Any work falling outside the included maintenance scope will be quoted separately and must be approved by the client before work begins.

6. Website Hosting & Resource Limits

  • Website hosting is included in the monthly subscription.
  • Hosting is subject to the allocated server resources, storage capacity, bandwidth and acceptable-use policies of the hosting environment.
  • If a website experiences unusually high traffic, excessive storage usage or resource consumption beyond normal hosting limits, Zapify Designs reserves the right to recommend an appropriate hosting upgrade.
  • Any additional hosting costs or upgrades will be communicated to the client and approved before additional charges are applied.
  • Zapify Designs will make reasonable efforts to maintain website availability and security but cannot guarantee uninterrupted hosting due to circumstances outside its control.

7. General Conditions

  • The R650 monthly subscription is a recurring service and must be paid according to the agreed billing arrangement.
  • Services included in the subscription are only available while the account remains active and payments are up to date.
  • Additional services not specifically included in the subscription may be quoted separately.
  • Zapify Designs reserves the right to update these terms, with reasonable notice to affected clients.

TERMS AND CONDITIONS APPLY.

By subscribing to this service, the client acknowledges and agrees to the applicable subscription terms and conditions.

The minimum subscription period and cancellation notice period are set out in each client's subscription agreement.
