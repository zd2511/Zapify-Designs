FAQ

Questions people ask before starting.

Straight answers about websites, SEO, software, pricing and working with Zapify Designs.

How much does a website cost?

Pricing starts at R800 for a landing page. Larger websites and custom work are quoted based on scope.

How long does a website take?

Timing depends on page count, content, revisions and features. A quote can include an estimated delivery window.

Do you build websites from scratch?

Yes. New websites can be designed and developed from scratch.

Can you redesign an existing website?

Yes. Existing sites can be redesigned, repaired, optimized or migrated depending on the setup.

Do you provide hosting?

Zapify can help with hosting setup and deployment; the hosting arrangement depends on the project.

Can you register a domain?

Yes. Zapify can help with domain registration, DNS and connecting the domain.

Do you provide SEO?

Yes. Services include technical SEO, on-page setup, sitemaps, indexing setup and local foundations.

Can you help with Google?

Yes. Zapify can help with Search Console, Bing Webmaster Tools, sitemaps and Google Business Profile setup.

Do you offer website maintenance?

Yes. Maintenance can cover updates, fixes, performance, security checks and ongoing support.

Can you build an online store?

Yes. E-commerce sites can be built around the products, checkout and management requirements.

Can you build custom software?

Yes. Custom web applications and business software are available.

Can you build dashboards?

Yes. Admin dashboards, reporting systems, customer portals and business dashboards can be built.

Can you integrate APIs?

Yes. API integrations can be included where the external service provides a suitable API.

Can you build AI tools?

Yes. AI interfaces, assistants, automations and API-connected AI features can be built where the required service is available.

Can you add WhatsApp functionality?

Yes. WhatsApp links, click tracking and other WhatsApp-related features can be included.

Do you work with businesses outside Vanderbijlpark?

Yes. Work is not limited to one town. Scope and communication can be handled remotely.

Do you work with clients throughout South Africa?

Yes. Zapify works with clients throughout South Africa.

Can you build a website for an individual or personal project?

Yes. Personal portfolios, projects and other individual websites can be built.

How do I request a quote?

Use the quote page and describe what you need, your timeline and budget if known.
