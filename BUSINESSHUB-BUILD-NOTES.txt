Zapify BusinessHub — rebuilt October 2026

BusinessHub is now a fully interactive local-first workspace rather than a demo dashboard.

Modules:
- Dashboard with live calculations from saved records
- Invoice maker with line items, VAT, discounts, payment status, live preview and downloadable PDF
- Quote maker with live preview, downloadable PDF and quote-to-invoice conversion
- Logo upload and business stamp upload; uploaded images are compressed and embedded in generated PDFs
- Customer management
- Expense tracking
- Monthly budget and category analysis
- General calculator, VAT calculator, profit calculator, markup/margin, break-even and budget variance
- Business profile and bank/payment details

Storage:
- localStorage only; no Supabase or Yoco dependency
- uploaded branding is stored in the current browser

Zapify Bot:
- global chat UI is injected by js/site.js on public pages and BusinessHub
- POST /api/chat is served by local-server.js
- OPENAI_API_KEY must be supplied as a server environment secret
- no API key is stored in frontend source
