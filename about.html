OUR STORY

About VTS Energy & Security

Volt Tech Solutions (Pty) Ltd, trading as VTS Energy & Security, is a Southern African company with a strong operational presence in South Africa and a growing footprint across the region. We provide integrated energy and digital security solutions for commercial, industrial, and residential clients.

We serve clients across South Africa, Zimbabwe, Zambia, Botswana, Namibia, Mozambique, Lesotho, Eswatini, and Malawi.

ENERGY SOLUTIONS

Keep operations powered.

We specialise in solar power installations, backup battery systems, UPS units, inverter systems, load shedding protection, generator integration, and energy audits.

Our solutions are designed to keep businesses operational despite power challenges common across the region.

DIGITAL SECURITY

Keep what matters protected.

We offer cybersecurity services, network security, data protection, CCTV surveillance, access control systems, biometric security, alarm systems, and remote monitoring.

Our team conducts security audits and vulnerability assessments to safeguard your business against modern threats.
