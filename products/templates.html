Template FAQ

Questions about buying and customising

Can I customise a Zapify website template?

Yes. Supported website template controls let you change colours, business wording and up to five preview images before purchase.

How do template purchases work?

Choose a template, customise it, enter your customer details at checkout, pay through the secure Yoco hosted checkout, and wait for server-side payment confirmation.

Can I change the images in a template?

Yes. Website templates include five image slots that can be replaced in the live preview. Digital Scrapbooks include five images by default and additional images cost R15 each.

How much do additional scrapbook images cost?

Five scrapbook images are included. Each additional image costs R15.

How much do scrapbook custom features cost?

Scrapbook feature prices vary by feature and are shown individually in the live editor. The final total is recalculated on the server before payment.

Can I request changes that are not in the customiser?

Yes. Larger or bespoke changes can be discussed separately and quoted according to scope.
