A few useful answers

Good to know.

How do I choose a website package?

Send us a short description of your business and what you need. We'll discuss the project scope and confirm which package is the best fit before work begins.

What's included in the R650 monthly plan?

Website hosting, first-year domain registration, business email and unlimited reasonable website maintenance are included, subject to the subscription terms. Mailbox and storage allocations are agreed when subscribing.

Can I move my website to another host?

Yes. If you move away from Zapify hosting, website files and/or transfer assistance are provided at no additional cost, subject to settling outstanding amounts and third-party licensing restrictions.

Do you work with agencies and freelancers?

Yes. Zapify Designs considers white-label work, overflow projects, subcontracting and creative or technical collaborations on a project-by-project basis.
