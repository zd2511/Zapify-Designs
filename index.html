FAQ

Questions?
We've Got Answers.

Here are some of the questions clients commonly ask before starting a website project with Zapify Designs.

What type of websites do you build?

We build custom business websites, service websites, portfolios, landing pages, e-commerce websites, booking websites and more. The design and functionality are shaped around the business rather than forcing every project into the same template.

Are your websites mobile-friendly?

Yes. Responsive design is part of the build, so the website is designed to work across phones, tablets, laptops and desktop screens.

Do you provide SEO?

Yes. Our website builds can include technical SEO foundations such as page titles, meta descriptions, semantic structure, responsive performance, clean URLs, structured data where appropriate and search-engine-ready content structure.

Can you add WhatsApp, Google Maps and social media?

Yes. WhatsApp, Google Maps, social profiles, contact forms and other useful integrations can be included according to the package and project requirements.

Can you build an online store or booking system?

Yes. E-commerce, booking flows, enquiry systems and other custom functionality can be built when your project needs more than a standard informational website.

What does full-stack development mean?

Full-stack development covers the systems behind a website as well as the visible interface. That can include databases, authentication, admin dashboards, APIs, customer portals and custom business logic. These are available as custom development when required.

How long does a website take to build?

Timing depends on the number of pages, content, design complexity and functionality. Once we understand your requirements, we can give you a realistic project timeline.

Do you provide hosting and domain setup?

We can assist with deployment and launch. Domain, hosting and paid third-party services are handled according to your chosen setup and may be quoted separately where applicable.
