ABOUT ZAPIFY DESIGNS

A Website Should
Feel Like You.

Zapify Designs is built around a simple idea: your website should not feel like something copied from a template. It should feel like your business — your style, your personality and the experience you want your customers to have.

We are web designers and developers who care about both sides of the experience: how a website looks and how it works. We take the time to understand what you are trying to achieve, then turn that idea into a polished, responsive website that is clear, memorable and easy to use.

And when your website needs more than what visitors see, we can take care of that too — from forms, bookings, e-commerce and integrations to databases, admin dashboards and other custom development. Those are tools we use to make the website work harder for you, not labels that define what Zapify Designs is.

FAQ

Questions clients ask before starting.

Can you build more than the website visitors see?

Yes. Our focus is web design and development, but when a project needs more behind the scenes, we can also build or connect backend systems such as databases, authentication, APIs, admin dashboards, booking logic and integrations.

Can you build an admin dashboard?

Yes. An admin dashboard can let authorised staff manage products, images, categories, bookings, enquiries, users and other business data without editing the website code directly.

Can you connect WhatsApp, bookings, maps or databases?

Yes. Depending on the project, websites can connect to WhatsApp, forms, booking workflows, Google Maps, email, databases, authentication providers, storage and third-party APIs.

Do you provide SEO and AEO?

We build technical SEO foundations and answer-friendly content structures, including semantic HTML, metadata, internal links, structured data where appropriate and clear question-and-answer content. Search rankings and inclusion in answer systems are not guaranteed.

Do you maintain websites after launch?

Yes. Maintenance can include updates, content changes, bug fixes, performance checks, image optimisation, security-minded maintenance and ongoing improvements.
