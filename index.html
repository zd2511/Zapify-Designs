ABOUT ZAPIFY DESIGNS

Good technology should make things clearer, not harder.

Zapify Designs started from a simple idea: a small business should not need a huge team to have a professional digital presence.

We work with businesses and individuals who have something they want to build, fix or improve. Sometimes that is a first website. Sometimes it is a redesign, an online store, a dashboard or a custom tool that does not exist yet.

We care about the practical side of digital work — clear communication, sensible technology, accessible interfaces and software that solves the actual problem.

Websites should solve problems, not simply look pretty. And when the project needs more than a website, we can build the systems around it too.

FAQ

Questions people ask before starting.

Clear answers, without the sales-script language. If you have a question that is not here, ask the Zapify Assistant or send an enquiry.

How much does a website cost?

Website packages start at R800 for a landing page. The Starter package is R1,250, Premium is R2,100 and Professional is R3,500. Larger or more specialized systems are quoted based on scope.

How long does a website take?

It depends on page count, content, revisions and functionality. A simple site can be much quicker than a custom application. We agree a realistic timeline before development starts.

Do you build websites from scratch?

Yes. Websites can be designed and developed from scratch, including responsive frontend work, custom interactions, forms, integrations and backend functionality when required.

Can you redesign an existing website?

Yes. We can redesign, repair, optimize or migrate an existing site. We can also preserve useful content and functionality instead of throwing everything away.

Do you provide hosting and domains?

We can assist with domain registration, DNS, hosting setup, deployment, SSL/HTTPS and business email configuration. Hosting costs themselves depend on the provider and plan.

Do you provide SEO and help with Google?

Yes. SEO work can include technical setup, metadata, content structure, sitemaps, Search Console, Bing Webmaster Tools, local SEO foundations and monitoring. No package guarantees a specific ranking.

Can you build an online store?

Yes. E-commerce can include product catalogues, categories, enquiry or checkout flows, payment integrations where supported, order management and admin functionality.

Can you build custom software and dashboards?

Yes. Projects can include customer portals, CRM-style systems, inventory tools, reporting dashboards, admin panels, database-driven applications and APIs.

Can you build AI tools and automation?

Yes. We can build structured AI interfaces, chatbots, automated workflows, lead capture, email automation and custom business tools. Any external AI provider or API needs to be configured separately.

Can you add WhatsApp functionality?

Yes. WhatsApp links, click tracking, enquiry flows and suitable automation can be integrated into a website.

Do you work with businesses outside Vanderbijlpark?

Yes. Zapify Designs can work remotely with clients throughout South Africa. Location-based SEO is used only where the business can genuinely serve that area.

Can you build a website for an individual or personal project?

Yes. Portfolios, personal sites, creative projects, blogs, landing pages and other individual projects are welcome.

How do I request a quote?

Use the quote form below, choose what you need and describe the project. You can also contact Zapify Designs on WhatsApp at 074 389 9657 or by email at zapifydesigns@gmail.com.
