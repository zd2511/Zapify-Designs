Quick answers

What is Zapify?

Clear answers to the questions visitors and answer engines need to understand before choosing a website service.

What does Zapify do?

Zapify Designs provides website design and development, SEO foundations, maintenance, automation, AI integrations and custom software.

How do Zapify templates work?

You choose a template, customise its content, colour preset, images and optional features, review the live preview, then continue to secure Yoco checkout.

How much are additional images?

Five images are included with a template. Each image beyond five is charged at R15, with the final amount recalculated server-side before payment.
